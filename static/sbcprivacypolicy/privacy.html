Privacy Policy

SBC Planner (formerly SBC Rating Calculator) · Effective 2026-09-20

SBC Planner is an app built by Guilherme Miranda and published on Google Play as com.guilhermealbm.sbcratingcalculator. It works out the cheapest way to complete a rating-only EA FC Squad Building Challenge, using the fodder you already own. It is provided free of charge and as is.

The short version:

  • No account, no sign-in, no EA credentials. There is nothing to log in to and nothing to connect.
  • I collect nothing about you. No analytics, no crash reporting in the app, no advertising, no trackers, no cookies.
  • What you enter stays on your device and is never uploaded.

Information I collect

None. The app asks for no personal information, has no user accounts, and sends nothing you enter anywhere. I do not receive, store, sell or share any personal information about you, because I never have any in the first place.

What the app stores on your device

So that your setup is still there next time you open it, the app saves the following in its own private storage on your device:

  • the ratings and number of cards you list in My club;
  • any prices you type into the prices editor;
  • the platform you chose, Console or PC;
  • whether automatic price updates are on, and whether you have already seen the app's introduction;
  • the most recent price list the app downloaded, described below.

None of this is sent to me, and none of it identifies you.

The app's one network request

To cost plans against current market prices, the app downloads a published price list: a static file at https://prices.guilhermealbm.com/sbc/v1/fc26.json. That is the only request the app makes on its own, and access to the internet for it is the only permission the app needs.

  • When. When you open the app, at most once an hour, and whenever you press Refresh in the prices editor. You can switch the automatic check off under Settings → Prices → Update prices automatically.
  • What it sends. A plain request for that one file. No account, no device identifier, no query parameters, and nothing about you, your club or your squads.
  • What the host can see. The file is served by Amazon Web Services (S3 behind CloudFront). As with a request to any website, the hosting provider can record standard access information such as your IP address, the time, and which file was requested. I do not use that to identify you or to build any profile of you, and I do not combine it with anything else.

The app works without the network. With no signal, or with the automatic check off, it plans against the last list it downloaded, or against the prices it was shipped with.

Links that open your browser

The app's settings screen links to my website and to this page. Tapping one hands the address to your browser, which then makes an ordinary web request. The app sends nothing along with it.

Backups made by your device

Android's own backup feature can include the app's stored data, listed above, in the backup of your device held in your own Google account, and restore it when you set up a new device. That backup is made by Android rather than by the app, you control it in your device's settings, and I have no access to it.

Google Play

The app is distributed through Google Play. Google collects its own information about installs and about your use of the Play Store, under Google's privacy policy, which I do not control. Google also shows me aggregated statistics in the Play Console, such as how many devices have the app installed, and anonymous crash reports collected by Android itself from users who chose to share diagnostics with Google. These come from Google, not from code in the app, and they do not identify you.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal information from anyone, so there is none collected from a child either. If you are a parent or guardian with a question about this, please get in touch at the address below.

Keeping and deleting your data

Everything the app saves is on your device, and it stays there until you remove it:

  • Settings → Clear club data empties your club inventory, and the confirmation it shows offers to clear the prices you typed as well.
  • Uninstalling the app removes what it stored on your device. If Android backup is switched on, a copy can remain in your Google account until it is removed there; you can delete it from your Google account's backup settings.

I hold no copy of any of it, so there is nothing for me to delete and no request you need to make of me.

Security

Your club, your prices and your plans live in the app's private storage, which Android keeps separate from other apps, and they are protected by whatever protects your device. Because nothing is transmitted to me and nothing is stored on a server of mine, there is no account of yours to be breached. No method of electronic storage is completely secure, and I cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

If what the app does changes, this page changes with it, and the date at the top says when the current version took effect. The page stays at this address, because copies of the app already installed link to it. This version replaces the one effective 2022-08-03, which was written for the app's earlier release.
